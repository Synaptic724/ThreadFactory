# Contributing to ThreadFactory

Thank you for your interest in ThreadFactory!

**ThreadFactory** is a specialized Python library designed for high-performance concurrent task management. It is focused on research, experimentation, and internal development of scalable threading systems, queue orchestration, and adaptive scheduling mechanisms.

---

## 🚫 Contributions

At this time, **ThreadFactory is not accepting external contributions**.  
This includes pull requests, feature requests, and unsolicited patches.

This decision is intentional to ensure architectural consistency and maintain alignment with the project's long-term design goals.

---

## 🧠 Feedback & Questions

While we are not open to code contributions, we **welcome respectful discussions** and **bug reports** that help improve the project or raise awareness of edge cases.  
Feel free to open an issue if you believe something is broken or could be improved from a usage perspective.

---

## 📜 Code of Conduct

Please review our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before engaging in any project discussions. We are committed to maintaining a respectful and inclusive environment.

---

Thank you for respecting the direction of the project.
